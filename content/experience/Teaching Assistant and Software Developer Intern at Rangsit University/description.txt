I served as a Teaching Assistant and Software Developer Intern at Rangsit International College, Rangsit University from August 2024 to December 2025. This role was a significant part of my academic and professional development journey.

I received [image:teaching_assistant.png:recognition] for my outstanding contribution as a teaching assistant and software engineer. During this time, I worked for an additional 4 months as part of my Co-Op Education process for RIC.

## Key Responsibilities

- Built backend services for internal systems, including a QR-based attendance system, job fair platform, and an alumni website (demo phase)
- Assisted in teaching and supporting students in computer science courses
- Collaborated with faculty and students on various software projects
- Provided technical guidance and mentorship to fellow students

## Projects Developed

I developed [image:jobfair_homepage.png:the RSU Jobfair System], [image:attendence_system.png:RIC Attendance System], and [image:ICT_courseplanner.png:the ICT Course Planner].

### RIC Attendance System

Implemented QR attendance with location verification, saving approximately 15 minutes per staff member per class by reducing manual tracking.

### RSU Jobfair System

Developed a job fair system used by ~300 students, staff, and participating companies during a live event and designed for reuse.

### ICT Course Planner

Built a course recommendation system adopted by ~100 students to support data-driven course selection.
