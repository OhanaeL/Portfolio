In November 2025, I participated in an open house project for Rangsit University, where I developed the Computer Vision Games project. This interactive project showcased camera-based games using hand gesture detection, demonstrating practical applications of computer vision technology.

The project was featured at the RIC Openhouse event, where visitors could interact with the games and experience computer vision technology firsthand. It was a great opportunity to showcase my skills in computer vision, Python, and OpenCV while contributing to the university's open house activities.
