"Did you know?" is a corporate social responsibility (CSR) project developed by Team Green Pie, aimed at raising awareness and making a positive community impact.

This project represents our commitment to social responsibility and community engagement through technology and education.

## Project Mission
- Raise awareness on important social issues
- Create positive community impact
- Promote social responsibility
- Educate and inform the community

## Key Features
- Educational content delivery
- Community engagement tools
- Awareness-raising mechanisms
- Social impact measurement

## Impact
This CSR project taught me the importance of using technology for social good and how developers can contribute to positive community change.

Working with Team Green Pie on this project reinforced my belief in the power of technology to create positive social impact and community engagement.
