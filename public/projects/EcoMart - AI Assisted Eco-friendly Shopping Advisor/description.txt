# Eco-mart - AI Assisted Eco-friendly Shopping Advisor

Eco-mart is an innovative shopping advisor application that won **2nd Runner-Up** at the Hack the Zodiac Hackathon 2024. The system helps users make environmentally conscious purchasing decisions by searching across multiple e-commerce platforms and calculating eco-friendliness ratings.

## Project Overview

Eco-mart addresses the growing need for sustainable shopping by providing users with an AI-powered advisor that evaluates products based on their environmental impact. The system searches across major e-commerce platforms and recommends the most eco-friendly options.

## Key Features

- **Multi-Platform Product Search**: Searches Amazon, Lazada, and other e-commerce platforms simultaneously
- **Eco-Friendliness Rating System**: Calculates environmental impact ratings for products
- **Intelligent Recommendations**: Recommends top 5 products based on user preferences and eco-friendliness filters
- **User Preference Filtering**: Allows users to customize search criteria and priorities

## Hackathon Achievement

This project was awarded **2nd Runner-Up** at the Hack the Zodiac Hackathon 2024, recognizing its innovation in combining AI technology with environmental consciousness.

## Technologies & Skills

- Web Scraping & Crawling
- AI/ML Algorithms
- E-commerce API Integration
- Environmental Impact Analysis
- Recommendation Systems

## Impact

Eco-mart demonstrates how technology can empower consumers to make more sustainable choices, contributing to environmental conservation through informed purchasing decisions.
